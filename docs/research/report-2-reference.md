Cyril Handyman & Door LLC — Website Rebuild & Local SEO Strategy
Research brief · November 3, 2026 · Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas
Executive summary
My central recommendation is to position Cyril Handyman & Door as a premium residential improvement and installation company, not a generic handyman business.

The website should communicate three things almost immediately:
1. Craftsmanship: Real completed projects, detailed installation footage, before-and-after transformations, and close-up workmanship.
2. Capability: Five clearly defined service divisions, each with its own substantial service page.
3. Trust: Verifiable business information, real customer feedback, clear estimates, service coverage, and documented project experience.
The strategy should be built around the following principles:
- One strong website, not 40 thin location pages. Build five excellent service pages and support them with real project stories.
- Make the portfolio a competitive advantage. Most contractors say they do good work. Cyril can demonstrate it through original photography and video.
- Do not compete solely on the word handyman. Garage doors, window coverings, patio screens, media walls, and holiday lighting have distinct customer searches and buying intentions.
- Treat Google Maps and organic search as separate but connected acquisition channels. The website needs to convert visitors, while the Google Business Profile needs to establish relevance, proximity, and prominence.
- Use the service-area business model correctly. Keep the residential address hidden. Do not create artificial business locations in Dallas, Plano, Frisco, or other cities.
- Build a project-based content system. A completed installation should become a gallery entry, a detailed project page where appropriate, social content, and a source of genuine customer reviews.
- Use video selectively. One hero video, compressed project footage, responsive still-image fallbacks, and deliberate loading priorities are preferable to a video-heavy site that damages mobile performance.
- Build for conversions, not just rankings. Prominent phone/contact actions, concise estimates, project examples, and service-specific calls to action should be present throughout.
- Make the design feel like an established craft business. Avoid generic contractor templates, AI-generated visual effects, excessive gradients, and repetitive stock-style layouts.
Research distinction: Verified facts are identified as such; strategic recommendations and design judgments are explicitly presented as recommendations. Competitor rankings, search volumes, and performance measurements are not treated as verified unless a source supports them.
